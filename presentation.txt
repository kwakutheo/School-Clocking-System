**Section 1: The Problem This System Solves**
- Many schools still depend on notebooks, paper files, WhatsApp messages, Excel sheets, or verbal reporting.
- These methods create common problems:
- Staff can sign for each other.
- Attendance records can be incomplete.
- It is hard to know who is in school right now.
- Payroll and attendance checking take too much time.
- Leave records can be confusing.
- There can be disputes about lateness, early leaving, or absence.
- Management may not have one clear source of truth.
- This system solves that by bringing everything together into one connected platform.

**Section 2: What The Whole System Is**
- The system has `two main sides`.
- The first side is the `mobile app` for staff.
- The second side is the `dashboard` for school management and administrators.
- These two sides work together in real time.

**Simple Explanation Of The Whole Flow**
- A staff member arrives at school.
- They use the mobile app to clock in.
- The system checks whether they are really at the school location or uses the school QR code.
- Their attendance record appears on the dashboard.
- Management can immediately see who is present, late, on break, or missing.
- At the end of the day, the same system can produce reports, summaries, and professional records.

**Section 3: Who Uses The System**
- `Staff members`
- They use the mobile app to clock in, clock out, manage breaks, check attendance history, request leave, view reports, and update their profile.
- `School administrators`
- They use the dashboard to monitor live attendance, manage staff, manage branches, shifts, holidays, permissions, leave approvals, and reports.
- `Higher-level supervisors or central administrators`

**Section 4: Mobile App Training - The Staff Side**
- This section teaches what a staff member sees and does daily.

**4.1 Home Screen**
- The mobile app home screen is the staff member’s daily control center.
- It brings together attendance actions, personal status, reminders, and school-related updates.
- From the code, the mobile app includes:
- Home
- History
- Report
- Profile
- Permissions and Leaves
- Calendar
- This means the staff member is not just clocking in.
- They are interacting with their work record in one place.

**4.2 What Staff Do First**
- A staff member logs into the app with their credentials.
- Once inside, they can immediately see their main dashboard.
- This home area is designed to give quick access to attendance actions and useful information about their workday.

**4.3 Clock In**
- When a staff member arrives, they tap `Clock In`.
- The system checks important conditions before recording the entry.
- In simple terms, it checks:
- Is today a working day?
- Is the person allowed to clock in?
- Are they at the correct branch or school location?
- Are they trying to clock in twice by mistake?
- If all is fine, the clock-in is accepted and saved.

**4.4 Clock Out**
- At the end of the workday, the staff member taps `Clock Out`.
- If they try to leave earlier than expected, the app does not silently allow it.
- It gives a warning and asks for confirmation.
- This is important because it reduces accidental errors and also helps management track early departures properly.

**4.5 Break Start and Break End**
- The system supports break tracking.
- A staff member can start a break and end a break.
- This matters because it gives a more complete picture of staff movement during working hours.
- It is especially useful in schools where breaks, lunch periods, and duty schedules matter.

**4.6 QR Scan Option**
- The system supports QR scanning from the mobile app.
- This means if GPS is difficult, weak, or inconvenient at a certain point, the school can still provide a secure attendance method using the branch QR code.
- The QR scan page checks whether the scanned code is a valid school branch code before accepting it.
- This helps prevent misuse.

**4.7 GPS and Location Awareness**
- The mobile app uses location to help confirm whether a person is within the allowed school area.
- In simple language:
- The app checks whether the person is close enough to the official school location.
- If they are too far away, the system can reject the attendance attempt.
- This protects the school from off-site clock-ins.

**4.8 Better Accuracy Handling**
- The mobile app tries to get the best possible location reading instead of blindly accepting poor GPS.
- If the signal is weak, it can try again and use the best reading available.
- This is important because it balances fairness with security.
- The system is not trying to punish people when GPS is poor.
- It is trying to get the most reliable answer possible.

**4.9 Fake Location Protection**
- The app checks whether the phone is using a fake or mocked location.
- In plain English:
- If someone tries to trick the system by pretending they are at school when they are not, the app can detect that and reject the action.
- This is one of the strongest trust features in the system.

**4.10 Offline Support**
- If internet is unavailable, some attendance actions can still be saved offline and synced later.
- This is excellent for areas where internet can be unstable.
- Instead of losing attendance data, the system keeps it and syncs it automatically when connection returns.
- That means the school does not have to stop operating because of temporary network problems.

**4.11 Attendance History**
- Staff can view their own attendance history.
- They can see records like:
- Clock in
- Clock out
- Break start
- Break end
- Whether a record is already synced or still pending
- This gives staff transparency.
- It reduces arguments because they can see their own record directly.

**4.12 Personal Reports**
- Staff can access their personal report section.
- This allows them to see their own attendance performance in a clear way.
- It supports transparency and self-management.

**4.13 Leave Requests**
- Staff can request leave through the app.
- This means they do not always have to rely on paper forms and informal back-and-forth messages.
- They enter:
- Leave type
- Start date
- End date
- Reason
- The system then keeps the request in a structured way for review.

**4.14 Calendar View**
- Staff can also view the school calendar area.
- This helps them know official terms, breaks, and schedules.
- That means staff can better understand when work is expected and when it is not.

**4.15 Profile and Personal Management**
- Staff can manage their own profile details.
- This keeps personal records more organized and reduces dependence on office staff for small updates.

**4.16 Notifications and Reminders**
- The mobile side includes notifications and reminders.
- For example:
- Reminder to clock out
- Shift reminders
- Silent syncing and refresh events
- This makes the app more helpful, not just a passive tool.

**Section 5: Dashboard Training - The School Management Side**
- This is the main control center for administrators.

**5.1 Dashboard Overview**
- The dashboard gives management a live picture of staff attendance and activity.
- Instead of waiting until the end of the day or month, they can see what is happening now.
- This is one of the biggest strengths of the system.

**5.2 Live Attendance Feed**
- The dashboard has a live attendance feed.
- It shows attendance events as they happen.
- Management can see:
- Who clocked in
- Who clocked out
- Which branch they attended from
- What time they recorded the action
- GPS coordinates where available
- Whether the action involved lateness, early departure, or admin override
- This creates visibility and immediate control.

**5.3 Late and Early Out Flags**
- The system automatically recognizes when someone is late or when someone leaves early.
- It does not require manual calculations from office staff.
- This reduces human error and saves time.

**5.4 Excusing Lateness**
- Sometimes lateness is genuine.
- The dashboard allows administrators to excuse lateness.
- Instead of the staff member remaining permanently marked late, the record can be updated to reflect that the lateness was officially excused.
- This adds fairness and professionalism.

**5.5 Excusing Early Departure**
- The same principle applies to early departure.
- If someone leaves early for a valid reason, the admin can officially mark the early departure as excused.
- That means the system is firm, but still human.

**5.6 Manual Clocking By Admin**
- The dashboard also allows `Manual Clock`.
- This is important for situations like:
- A staff member’s phone is dead
- A phone is lost
- A genuine technical issue prevented normal clocking
- A record needs to be entered for a valid reason
- The admin can add the attendance manually.
- The system marks that it was an admin override.
- This keeps everything transparent.

**5.7 Attendance Overview And Charts**
- The dashboard shows summary information and charts.
- This helps management answer questions like:
- How many people attended today?
- How many are currently on site?
- What time do most people arrive?
- Which staff still have attendance issues?
- It turns raw attendance data into something leadership can understand quickly.

**5.8 Attendance Report Page**
- There is a dedicated report area where the school can:
- View monthly reports
- View term reports
- View academic year reports
- Export reports to PDF
- Export bulk reports for many staff at once
- Filter by school branch
- Show only exceptions if needed
- This makes the system useful not only daily, but also weekly, monthly, termly, and yearly.

**5.9 Employee Search And Selection**
- The report section allows quick selection of staff.
- This is important for HR, heads of department, and school leadership who need specific staff records without wasting time.

**Section 16: Security Features**
- This section is important because clients need to trust the system.

**16.1 Login Protection**
- Users must log in with credentials.
- This ensures attendance is tied to the right person.

**16.2 Biometric Support**
- The mobile app can use biometric verification in some attendance actions.
- In simple terms, the phone can ask the user to confirm identity with fingerprint or device authentication.
- This adds another layer of protection.

**16.3 GPS Boundary Protection**
- The system checks whether the user is within the allowed branch area.
- This helps stop off-site attendance fraud.

**16.4 QR Validation**
- The system validates branch QR codes.
- Not just any QR code will work.
- This protects against casual misuse.

**16.5 Fake GPS Detection**
- The app checks for mocked location use.
- This is very important in real-life attendance control.

**16.6 Time Tampering Protection**
- The system also protects against phone time manipulation.
- In plain English:
- If someone changes the phone time to try to fool the system, the app can recognize that something is wrong.
- This is a major trust feature.

**16.7 Tenant Isolation / School Separation**
- For systems serving multiple schools, one school’s data is kept separate from another school’s data.
- This means School A should not be seeing School B’s records.
- For clients, this is very important for privacy and professionalism.

**16.8 Password-Protected Sensitive Actions**
