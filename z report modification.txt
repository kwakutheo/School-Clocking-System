You bring up an excellent architectural point. Real-world reporting must reflect the exact state of the organization *at that specific point in time*. 

If a school or employee did not exist (or was inactive/deleted) during the selected timeframe, showing them with a 0% score is fundamentally incorrect—they should be entirely omitted from that report.

Here is exactly how I plan to fix this in the backend to ensure it perfectly mirrors real life:

### 1. Fixing School Rankings (Institution Level)
Currently, the system iterates over all schools and if a school had 0 `expectedEmployeeDays` in the selected timeframe, it defaults to a 0% presence rate. 
**The Fix:** 
I will add a filter. If a school’s `expectedEmployeeDays` is exactly `0` for the selected timeframe (meaning no active employees were scheduled to work during those dates), **the school will be completely dropped from the returned list**. 
* **Real-life result:** If you look at "4 Weeks Ago" and a school hadn't been onboarded yet, it simply won't be in the ranking list at all.

### 2. Fixing Employee Rankings (Staff Level)
Currently, the `getEmployeeRankings` method calculates scores for every employee. If they were hired *after* the timeframe, their `expectedDays` is calculated as 0, which results in a 0% completion score.
**The Fix:**
Inside the loop that calculates employee metrics, I will add a check: if `expectedDays === 0` (meaning they were not hired yet, or they were suspended/deleted for the entirety of that specific timeframe), **I will `continue` and skip adding that employee to the results array.**
* **Real-life result:** If Kwame was hired 2 weeks ago, and you view the "4 Weeks Ago" ranking, Kwame will not be in the list.

### 3. Handling Deleted/Suspended Entities (The Time-Travel Rule)
Because you mentioned that schools and employees will be removed over time:
* If an employee was active 4 weeks ago, but is deleted/suspended *today*, they **should still appear** in the "4 Weeks Ago" report because they were active *then*. 
* The system's `EmployeeStatusLog` already tracks this history correctly, but I will ensure the filtering logic relies strictly on the historical `expectedDays > 0` metric, rather than checking if `employee.status === 'active'` *today*.

**Summary of the Code Change:**
In `saas-admin.service.ts`:
1. In `findAllTenants()`: Filter out any tenant where `metrics.expectedEmployeeDays === 0`.
2. In `getEmployeeRankingRows()`: Skip pushing any employee into the `results` array if `expectedDays === 0`.
